240.1: Snake Oil

a back-page advertisement in translation from a Borlish weekly in January 1897; the front page story of this issue reported that the Drengot Collusion had declared war on Vascony.

SOFREÐ VOS D'UN FEBILTAÐ NY MEMBR, un freidour ny gien ben un vogdeç de nis?
DO YOU SUFFER FROM A LETHARGY OF THE LIMBS, a chill in the cheeks or a vacancy of vigour?

Lor prenað, com faun oy mil a gent, un cogler cottidian de BAUSM SANDRIN DE SANAT VESCOMB — y remed celebr detaxionnað des y frugt luiç dell'Arcabil.
Then take, as thousands now do, a daily spoonful of DR VESCOMB'S SANDRINE SALVE—the celebrated restorator detacted from the bright fruits of the Arcabil.

Un soul dos de cogler pre desjeun aðel y sanc con anim, met roðabr ag fam, e starchisc y corp contr nossell'ec yarn norðer e moglastr.
A single spoonful before breakfast enlivens the blood, sharpens the appetite, and fortifies the constitution against our damp northern winters.

Y bausm de Vescomb es rannuðað pall'eç sanat dy Relicquat de Santað a Calder com idone tan por domn, por figl e poll'eç parsanant.
Vescomb's salve is recommended by medics of the Calder Museum of Health as suitable also for ladies, children, and the convalescent.

Façað prudenç de haval boncop vençað sou nom comportant: vray bottel de Vescomb moustrn l'ensign dy naðr e vign.
Beware of cheap imitations sold under similar names: genuine Vescomb bottles bear the emblem of the viper and vine.
