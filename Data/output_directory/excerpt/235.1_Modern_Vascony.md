235.1: Modern Vascony

notes in translation on the polity of Vascony [Aquitaine, N Spain] in recent centuries.

Vascony se mis com un rostr poll'Apprestanç Modest, oc stant un commouð cultural ny siecr ogtoç e novoç descreut pall'imposment diligendessem de cod a rigteç, y plurem cas l'autortað sur y famigl.
Vascony was at the vanguard of the Modest Arrangement, a social movement in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries characterised by more stringent enforcement of morality codes, especially with respect to familial powers.

Cos celebr, y scrittour Antòin Herrel (d'an aval Tony Smith) deye cos forçað enfugr de Vascony posc sell'attent por l'ouvr blasphem 'Scen Terren' editar.
Famously, author Antòin Herrel (latterly Tony Smith) was forced to flee Vascony to Kent after attempting to publish his blasphemous work 'Earthly Scene'.

Nell'enç, Vascony saveu skighar denir jannestað par y Var Theudesc Dovem ny dezein 1820.
At first, Vascony managed not to become embroiled in the Second German War of the 1820s.

Alcant ig periglað ern sell'envou ne Mendeva e nell'Est, interim, l'ançan rey Hernand IV manca alcun ouçon deur declar de var souvr contr y Concert Norðer e sociant con y Convey.
Once its interests in Mendeva and the East were threatened, however, then-king Hernand IV had no choice but to declare against the Northern Concert and in favour of the Convoy.

A plaç entarn dy siecr veint, Vascony mantene ligaçon starc cosogr sy vecin Provenç graç an enmigtað communal vars y Collujon Drengoçan tras lorry tarmn norðer.
Into the twentieth century, Vascony maintained strong ties with its neighbour Provence by virtue of a shared enmity towards the Drengot Collusion to their north.
