239.1: The Red Princess

continued translation from the beginning of [real-life] book The Henchmen of Zenda by KJ Charles, a 2018 pastiche of the 1894 romp The Prisoner of Zenda.

Deur cas y bustr de Sy Nobiltað Reyal y Princes Flavia, y quen a venir.
Except in the case of Her Royal Highness Princess Flavia, the future queen.

Flavia sta un cosvour (lon digr) dy fraðr, heriter sevent dy sanc posc y Prinç Rudolf.
Flavia was cousin (of course) to the brothers, next heir of the Blood after Prince Rudolf.

Sy paðr, ayent un pog may de sis ag majortað de sy famigl, aye Europ javigað por y rous imparent de linaç ottem seyon descovribr, e progentau un doncel san de tegnt idone e de nis admirabr.
Her father, having somewhat more sense than the majority of his family, had scoured Europe for the best-born unrelated redhead he could discover, and produced a healthy girl of appropriate colouring and admirable vigour.

La brouca tan de beltað com recas d'un princes vige, e may de çavel par un mesur signfeyant, e sy doum, jamay doutað den, sta y rey a venir mariðar, cuscon sey le.
She had as much beauty as a wealthy princess requires, and significantly more brain, and her destiny, never doubted, was to wed the future king, whoever he might be.

Jo say noc ou Rudolf Rous la desira por la meðes. Jo dout den; jo connuð noc ou candon le vis un femn com may d'un loy yon sy hopr ðejar, ben d'un mejan por sy fraðr vexar.
I don't know if Red Rudolf wanted her for herself. I doubt it; I am not aware he ever saw a woman as more than a place to put his prick, or a way to confound his brother.
