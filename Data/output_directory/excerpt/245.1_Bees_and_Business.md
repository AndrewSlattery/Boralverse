245.1: Bees and Business

minutes in translation from the annual general meeting of a village beekeepers' society in Furlain [Friuli].

Contris dy Raðunanç a Torn all'An dy Çaucr d'Aucoumer a Iemone, tenuð ny preu semman dell'an novel ny hal parroiçal d'an 1987 je Març 7.
Minutes of the New-Year Meeting of the Iemone [Gemona] Beekeepers' Society, held in the first week of the new year, in the parish hall on 1987 March 7.

Present: onç sodal eð y directour, Dn Ilaria Fadin. Deliquy fo receut de Sr Tonut.
Present: eleven members and the chairwoman, Mrs Ilaria Fadin. Apologies were received from Mr Tonut.

Y nombler referisceu y stal a miel ny feir automnal aye may gajannað all'oç de calscon annað preluyent.
The treasurer reported that the honey stall at the autumn fair had raised more than in any previous year.

Concordað fo l'ig y surcresc sey benoçað por un scon poiðour accatar, a star tenuð par y greffour e prestað ag sodal.
It was resolved that the surplus be spent on a second extractor, to be kept by the secretary and lent to members.

Sr Bertoli svoccau alcheðr y them dell'eç cavr de sy vecin, eç, lonc luy, sell'avegl vexant.
Mr Bertoli once again raised the matter of his neighbour's goats, which he believes are disturbing his bees.

Y directour le soumoneu y Çaucr n'a alcun autortað sur cavr.
The chairwoman reminded him that the Society has no authority over goats.

Un soumission por icon de vesp admettr ag cors annual fo contrdontað par nouf vouð contr treç.
A motion to admit photographs of wasps to the annual competition was defeated by nine votes to three.

Alcun affer ancour remanent, y raðunanç se closisceu all'hour nouf e demeg, e sodal forn convoccað a remanir por vin e vastel a miel.
There being no further business, the meeting closed at half past nine, and members were invited to stay for wine and honey cakes.
