237.1: Tommarth

notes in translation on the Merch city of Tommarth [Tamworth, Staffs].

Tonvrouð es y citað caver eð y maissem ne Marclont. Sy pouvlanç teyen a dessur de 500 murðon jusc d'an 2010.
Tommarth is the capital and largest city of Markland. It had a population of over 5 million people by the year 2010.

Y region souvau instançment pall'Angr ny siecr seg; ny siecr nouf lo sta denuð y citað primer dy Reyaum Markesc smargent. Lo receveu starchiscment signfeyant tras y siecr sousevent com bastegl contr y Cambresc all'ovest e soulor contr y Dannesc all'est.
The area was settled by the Angles in the sixth century; by the ninth it had become the primary city of the nascent Merch Kingdom. It was fortified significantly over the next centuries in defence against the Welsh to the west and then against the Danes to the East.

Nell'asc modern Tonvrouð brouc un doujug conveyer, chthonic eð effus dignant cos vecin y Dusker, ant quarvet ig inclus Kibbrough e Cathedral.
In the modern day Tommarth has an extensive underground rail system known locally as the Dusker, with stops including Kibbrough and Cathedral.

Y nom dy citað vien dy floy Tome yon lo se situ a cost. Paravant (pre y reform modern cas dreçment) stant dreçað Tomwarth, y region local er nommað ne Veðr Anglesc Tomworðig "cloist ben acr jos y Tome".
The city takes its name from the Tome river on which it stands. Formerly (before the modern spelling reform) spelt Tomwarth, the local area was known in Old English as Tomworðig "enclosure, field by the Tome".
