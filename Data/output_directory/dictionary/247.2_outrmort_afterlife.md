247.2: outrmort "afterlife"

outrmort /ˌu.tr̩ˈmɔrt/ [ˌu.tɐˈmɔːt]
- afterlife, hereafter, continued existence after death;
- heaven, underworld, netherworld, land of the dead, the place where the dead are believed to have gone;
- (archaeology) grave goods, the collection of objects buried with someone who has died;
- (in children's games) jail, out, the state of players who have been eliminated from a round or the location where they wait the round out

Etymology: Middle Borlish compound of outr- "beyond" < Latin ultrā and mort "death" < Latin mors, probably a calque of the Ecclesiastical Latin phrase vīta ultrā mortem "life beyond death". The sense of a place, rather than a state, appears in early modern translations of classical and Malchassian texts, where it renders the underworlds of pagan religions. The archaeological sense comes from nineteenth-century antiquarian catalogues, and the children’s-game sense is recorded from the early twentieth century.
