25.3: kerellabr "controversial"

kerellabr /ˌke.rɛˈla.br̩/ [ˌke.ʀɪˈla.bɐ]
- controversial, contentious, characterised by or liable to cause argument;
- disputed, arguable, open to debate with respect to validity or genuineness

Etymology: potential adjectival derivation from verb kerellar "to argue, debate, quarrel", itself derived from noun kerel "dispute, argument, quarrel". This descends from Old Borlish, borrowed from synonymous Old French querele, which reflects Latin querēla "complaint, grievance".
