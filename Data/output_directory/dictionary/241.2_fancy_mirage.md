241.2: fancy "mirage"

fancy /fanˈtsi/ [fɐnˈtsi]
- apparation, vision, wraith, hallucination, illusion, something seen which is not there;
- mirage, trick of the light, particular apparitions which are due to heat or exhaustion;
- pomp, pageantry, spectacle, an impressive display (usually not backed by anything substantive);
- pipe dream, castle in the air, a hope considered to be impossible

Etymology: a Middle Borlish contraction of fantasy "imagination, concept, idea". This descends from Latin phantasia "notion; phantom; imagination", which was borrowed from Greek φαντασίᾱ • phantasíā "appearance, image". The extension of the word to showy displays, first seen in philosophical Borlish in the eighteenth century, may reflect knowledge of the Greek etymon (which was used similarly).
