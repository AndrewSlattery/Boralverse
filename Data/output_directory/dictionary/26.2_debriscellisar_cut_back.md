26.2: debriscellisar "cut back"

debriscellisar /ˌde.briˌxɛ.liˈzar/ [ˌde.bʀɪˌçɛ.lɪˈzɐː]
- to prune, trim, to cut back overgrowing plants;
- to clear out undergrowth from an area;
- to lay the groundwork, set the stage, to clear the ground for an undertaking

Etymology: a Middle Borlish period derivation from briscel "shrub, bush", literally "cause to be rid of low-growing plants". This noun from earlier arbriscel, diminutive of arbr "tree". In extended use the verb is seen from the eighteenth century.
