235.4: issaughar "rinse, flush"

issaughar /ˌɪ.soˈjar/ [ˌɪ.sʊˈʝɐː]
- rinse, swill, to wash by pouring water over or through something;
- sluice, flush out, to clear a channel or a drain by a rush of water;
- irrigate, flood, to let water cover or suffuse land;
- leach, wash out, to drain something of its substance

Etymology: from Old Borlish issauȝar "drain, let water out of", via a synoymous exaquō "I drain" in Vulgar Latin from the Classical root noun aqua "water". Cognate with Tuscan sciacquare "rinse", whence the modern borrowing sciacquato "watercolour".
