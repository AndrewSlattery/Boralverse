227.2: keyottar "pamper"

keyottar /kjɔˈtar/ [kjʊˈtɐː]
- pamper, spoil, coddle, indulge, to treat with excessive care or leniency;
- cosset, mollycoddle, overprotect, to shield someone from hardship or worldly experience to an excessive degree;
- nurse, baby, treat gingerly, to handle a delicate object or smarting body part with exaggerated care;
- humour, pander to, to refrain from pushing back against [someone's] whims or demands

Etymology: Middle Borlish, diminutive formation from older keyar (also caiȝar) "look after, tend to". This descends from Vulgar Latin cavicō "take care of", frequentative derivation from Classical caveō "beware of, look out for".
