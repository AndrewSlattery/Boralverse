242.2: anathemat "allergy"

anathemat /aˌna.θeˈmat/ [ɐˌna.θɪˈmat]
- allergy, sensitivity, intolerance, an immune reaction to a usually harmless substance;
- rash, hives, flare-up, the visible bodily reaction itself;
- (historical) excommunication, anathema, a formal curse cutting someone off from a church;
- ban, boycott, blacklist, a collective refusal to deal with a person or a business;

Etymology: originally in religious use, a Middle Borlish borrowing from Latin anathema (stem anathemat-) "offering someone's life; excommunication", itself from Greek ανάθεμα • (anáthema) "votive offering", noun derivation of ἀνατίθημι • (anatíthēmi) "lay upon; dedicate". Extension to medical writing is seen from the seventeenth century, and has been used by the wider public from the nineteenth century.
