236.2: cancel "railing"

cancel /kanˈtsɛl/ [kɐnˈtsɛl]
- railing, banister, balustrade, a barrier of upright bars set along a steep drop or a staircase;
- lattice, grille, grate, grating, a screen of bars fitted in a window or door;
- (typography) deletion, redaction, any marking indicating that a string of characters should be ignored;
- hashtag, hash, pound sign, octothorpe, the symbol #

Etymology: direct descendant via Old Borlish cancel "grid of bars, metal fence" from synonymous cancellī in Latin. This is the diminutive plural of cancer "crab, lattice". The primary sense had shifted to safety railings by the Middle Borlish period; extension to typography arose in the sixteenth century by the practice of hatching out unwanted text.
