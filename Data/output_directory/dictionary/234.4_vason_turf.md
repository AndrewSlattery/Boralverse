234.4: vason "turf"

vason /vaˈzɔn/ [vɐˈzɔn]
- sod, turf, a cut block of earth held together by grassroots;
- peat, such blocks as a material for roofing or walling or as fuel;
- sward, green, lawn, the grassy surface of open ground;
- pitch, the field of play (especially in lineball)

Etymology: directly from Old Borlish vason "turf", borrowed from synoymous wason in Old French or directly from its Old Dutch source; either way from Classical German vasō "meadow, field". Later semantic developments appear to parallel and postdate those in Norman French, including (by the nineteenth century) its use in sports.
