247.4: pacentar "wait out"

pacentar /ˌpa.tsɛnˈtar/ [ˌpa.tsɪnˈtɐː]
- bear with, be patient with, to show forbearance towards;
- put up with, endure, to suffer a nuisance without complaint;
- wait out, ride out, sit out, to stay until something passes;
- sit with, keep vigil over, to stay at the bedside of a sick or dying person;
- (cookery) rest, prove, leave to stand, to let sit before the next stage of a recipe

Etymology: Middle Borlish verb derived from pacent "patient" < Latin patiēns, present participle of patior "I suffer, I endure". The earliest uses are in devotional writing, extended to storms and sieges in the early modern period, and to hospitals and cookery in the nineteenth century.
