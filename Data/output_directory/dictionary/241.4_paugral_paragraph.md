241.4: paugral "paragraph"

paugral /poˈgral/ [pʊˈgʀal]
- clause, article, a numbered section of a legal document (e.g. a contract or a statute);
- paragraph, a section of text set off from that which precedes and follows;
- pilcrow, a symbol (in older documents a bracket or stroke) placed in the margin to denote e.g. a new speaker or topic;
- string attached, catch, proviso, condition, stipulation, a (usually unwelcome) qualification of an offer

Etymology: from Old Borlish palgraf, pargrau "paragraph", alterations of synonymous paragraphus in Latin. Used for various scribal indications of new paragraphs since the fourteenth century. The final senses are from 1911 and arose from lawyers' informal use.
