235.3: seget "crop"

seget /seˈʒɛt/ [sɪˈʝɛt]
- crop, a cultivated plant grown for food or for trade;
- (specifically) cereal, a grass farmed for its edible grains;
- harvest, yield, what a season's growing brings in;
- any dense mass of upright things such as hair, spears or masts

Etymology: Middle Borlish borrowing from Latin seges "field sown with a cereal crop", used metonymically for the field's contents. Used metaphorically for masses which visually resemble standing crops, the word is especially common in military contexts.
