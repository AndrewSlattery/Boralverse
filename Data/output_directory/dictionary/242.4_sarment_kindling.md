242.4: sarment "kindling"

sarment /sarˈmɛnt/ [sɐːˈmɛnt]
- cutting, slip, shoot, a length cut from a vine or a shrub for planting;
- runner, stolon, a creeping stem which roots where it touches the ground;
- (with plural agreement) brushwood, kindling, twigs gathered for burning;
- cadet branch, offshoot, a junior line of a family;
- hanger-on, sponger, someone living off a richer relative or patron

Etymology: an unproblematic descendant via Old Borlish sarment "cutting, shoot" from Latin sarmentum "shoot", a nominal derivation of verb sarpō "I prune (a vine)". Possibly reinforced in the Middle Borlish as a reborrowing from botanical Latin. Genealogical senses are attested from the seventeenth century.
