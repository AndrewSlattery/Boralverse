218.3: focment "husbandry"

focment /fɔkˈmɛnt/ [fʊkˈmɛnt]
- breeding, (animal) husbandry, the management and care of farm animals by which quailties advantageous to humans are developed and perpetuated;
- curation, selection, the act of carefully choosing a particular subset of candidates for some purpose;
- nurture, upbringing, the environmental influences that contribute to a person's development

Etymology: noun derivative of focar "breed", a Middle Borlish borrowing from contemporary Willem focken "breed (animals)". This descends, possibly via Norrish, from Classical German fuccō "I strike, hit; I have sex with". Extension to other winnowing processes is seen from the sixteenth century; in the last senses it is contrasted with engin "nature, innate character".
