234.2: manoscarteyar "syncretise"

manoscarteyar /ˌma.no.xarˈtjar/ [ˌma.nʊ.xɐːˈtjɐː]
- to compose or arrange (a piece of music) using the manoscard scale;
- hybridise, cross, to blend two strains or styles into a new whole;
- syncretise, to merge elements of separate traditions into one practice;
- conflate, confuse, mix up, muddle, to incorrectly treat (distinct things) as interchangeable

Etymology: sporadic use in the Middle Borlish period as a derivation from manoscart "manoscard", a particular musical scale which developed near the Burgund town of Manosque in the thirteenth century. Other senses are a later rederivation from the root's extended meanings including "fusion, combination, blend". Use of the word with pejorative connotations is attested only from the mid-twentieth century.
