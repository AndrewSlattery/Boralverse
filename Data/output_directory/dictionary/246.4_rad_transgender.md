246.4: rad "transgender"

rad /rad/ [ʀad]
- trans, transgender, having a gender other than the one assigned at birth;
- characteristic of a particular culture of trans people (as in trans music, trans fashion, etc.);
- (biology, colloquially) sequentially hermaphroditic, changing sex during its life;
- (linguistics) exhibiting quirky gender agreement within a language's system of noun classes;
- retrofitted, repurposed, converted, (of e.g. buildings, ships or computer programs) having been altered or extended for another use

Etymology: late nineteenth century, from the tovaric scene of the Riverine Cities, borrowing a Slavone word, most likely Zahid Russian rado "gladly, willingly; on purpose, deliberately", from Proto-Slavic *ràdъ "glad, willing". The biological and linguistic senses are twentieth-century extensions in popular science writing and in grammarians' slang respectively; the last sense originates in estate agents' jargon of the 1980s, from which it spread to other converted things.
