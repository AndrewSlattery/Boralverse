239.4: belligrman "warmonger"

belligrman /bɛˌlajr̩ˈman/ [bɪˌla.jɐˈman]
- warmonger, hawk, one who agitates for war or stirs a quarrel toward it;
- arms dealer, war profiteer, one who trades in the means of making war;
- belligerent, a state or a party actively engaged in a war;
- (in apposition, of a polity) warring, bellicose, given to making war on its neighbours

Etymology: nineteenth century, compound of belligr "warlike, martial, valiant" (from synonymous belliger in Latin) and the suffix -man "merchant, purveyor". This suffix derives via -mang in Old Borlish, likely from Old English mangere "trader, merchant" or else directly from Latin mangō "(dishonest) dealer, especially of slaves". Originally in general attitudinal use; the more mercenary senses were influenced by other words using the -man suffix.
