238.4: magnfayent "magnificent"

magnfayent /ˌmɛjn.feˈjɛnt/ [ˌmɛiɱˈfjɛnt]
- splendid, sumptuous, magnificent, august, eminent, grand in appearance or in scale;
- munificent, generous, magnanimous, giving on a lavish scale;
- accomplished, impressive, having done great deeds;
- grandiose, overblown, more impressive in show than in substance

Etymology: late Old Borlish maȝn faȝent (also as one word), a calque adaptation of Latin magnificus "great, splendid" as maȝn "much, a lot" and faȝent "doing", from Latin magnus "big" and faciēns "doing, making". Unambiguously ironic or deprecatory usage is seen only from the late nineteenth century.
