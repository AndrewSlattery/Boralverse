246.2: quincarol "straight man"

quincarol /ˌkwɪn.kaˈrɔl/ [ˌkwɪŋ.kɐˈʀɔl]
- (archaic) ladies' man, gallant, womaniser, a man much given to the company or pursuit of women;
- (dialect) the only man in a household or a workplace of women;
- straight man, heterosexual man, a man attracted exclusively or predominantly to women;
- (modifier) straight, het, heterosexual, (of a man) attracted exclusively or predominantly to women;
- laddish, boorish, characteristic of stereotypical depictions of heterosexual men

(in the latter senses also quin /kwɪn/ [kwɪn])

Etymology: though current in the first sense since Middle Borlish, the word later survived mainly in dialect. It comes from a compound of Old Norrish kvinna "woman" and karl "man, fellow", literally "women's man" (compare Old Norrish kvennamaðr "womaniser"). It was reintroduced into standard Borlish via twentieth-century tovaric [queer] slang; the clipping quin is from the 1970s.
