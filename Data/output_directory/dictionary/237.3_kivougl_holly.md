237.3: kivougl "holly"

kivougl /kiˈvujl/ [kɪˈvwɪl]
- holly, an evergreen shrub with glossy spined leaves and scarlet berries;
- a sprig or a garland of this cut as a midwinter decoration;
- puzzle, jigsaw, a challenging diversion especially comprising an image cut into small pieces to be reassembled;
- riddle, enigma, something baffling or hard to understand

Etymology: via Old Borlish cuvoȝl "holly" and a Vulgar Latin form like *accufolius "holly" from the Classical adjective aquifolius "prickly-leaved". This is a compound of acus "needle" and folium "leaf". In the last senses the word is seen from the late nineteenth century, originally as a trade name coined by a Borlish subsidiary of the De Marnoshring toymaker's guild; the metaphorical extension appears only a couple of decades later.
