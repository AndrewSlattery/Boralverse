237.2: ottonam "brass"

ottonam /ˌɔ.toˈnam/ [ˌɔ.tʊˈnam]
- brassware, objects made of brass taken collectively as in a household or a harness;
- brass section, brass, the brass instruments of an ensemble taken together;
- brass band, a body of players on such instruments especially of a town or a military;
- blare, fanfare, a loud brazen burst of sound

Etymology: Middle Borlish, borrowed along with otton "brass" from Tuscan ottoname "brassware" and ottone "brass". This is a rebacketing of earlier lottone, borrowed from Arabic لَاطُون • lāṭūn "copper alloy", itself from a Classical Turk altūn "gold metal" that may be related to contemporary Chinese 銅 • dūng "copper". The musical senses developed through the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries.
