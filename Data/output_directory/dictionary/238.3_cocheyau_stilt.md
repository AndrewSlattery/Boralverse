238.3: cocheyau "stilt"

cocheyau /koˈkjo/ [kʊˈkjo]
- stilt, one of a pair of poles with footrests with which one can walk raised above the ground;
- pile, piling, one of the posts on which a building stands over water or soft ground;
- shank, pin, a long thin leg seeming too spindly for its owner;
- prop, crutch, trappings, something that lends a person height or standing they have not earned

Etymology: seventeenth century, in travel writing describing a particular acrobatic act in coastal China, borrowed from a local Chinese language's 高蹺 • cōchiāu "stilts", literally "high-tiptoe". Use for lower limbs may (if not self-directed) be seen as offensive. The last senses are attested from the 1920s, originating in entertainment circles.
