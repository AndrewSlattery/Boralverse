# Lingua questionnaire → Borlish reference grammar

A coverage checklist based on Comrie & Smith (1977), *Lingua Descriptive Studies: Questionnaire*, Lingua 42: 1–72. Online at <https://www.eva.mpg.de/lingua/tools-at-lingboard/questionnaire/linguaQ.php>.

Section numbers are the questionnaire's. The topic labels are short summaries, not the questions themselves, so keep the original open when writing a chapter. Tick an item once the grammar covers it, or says that it doesn't apply (*n/a*). A grammar should still mention an absence briefly, e.g. "nouns have no gender".
