# Irregular verbs: paradigms to fill in

Reconstructed 2026-10-01 from the glossed blocks in Scaunç (refs **S**n = Scaunç line) and the unglossed sentence file (refs **C**n = line in *borlish sentences with translations.txt*). This covers the verbs needed for *Copulas, Auxiliaries and Modal Verbs*, plus *fair*, *dar* and *savir* for the Verb Tables appendix.

**How to read the cells**
- **bold**: attested in the corpus.
- `form?`: my reconstruction from the regular patterns or from a related form. Please confirm or correct.
- `___`: no evidence and no safe guess. Please fill in.
- `—`: does not exist (e.g. imperative 1SG).
- Two forms separated by `/` are both attested. Please say which is current, or whether they differ in meaning.

Edit the cells in place. Delete the `?` once you're happy with a form. Each verb's notes end with specific questions (**Q**).

Non-finite: infinitive **star**, present participle **stant**, past participle **stað**.

Contractions: **jo's**, **tu's**, **le's / la's / lo's**, **no's**.

- The present subjunctive 1PL **seim** (S15945) is irregular. Regular verbs have *-eu/-au* there.
A: I believe that's a mistake on my part, or just blindly deriving from Classical Latin instead of considering the Vulgar paradigm. I've fixed it to "seyau".
- **Q1.** *Nos stau livað d'ableganç vars y maugr* 'We're fed up of being on the sidelines' (C2283). Is **stau** the imperfect 1PL (expected for an *-ar* verb), with a loose present-tense translation, or a present-tense variant?
A: That's another mistake; the Borlish should be "som", not "stau".
- **Q2.** *Ja sco ne vugl router* 'I'm still in a traffic jam' (S14409, glossed be.1s). Is **sco** an inverted contraction of *so jo*?
A: Yes, well spotted! There's a whole paradigm of these: sco, stu, zy; sonos, sovos, sony (you can see the 3s and 3p descend from an obsolete use of "y" for the 3rd-person subject in inversion after consonants).

## er (copula, imperfect only)

| | 1SG | 2SG | 3SG | 1PL | 2PL | 3PL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| imperfect | **er** | **er** | **er** | **erau** | **erað** | **ern** |

Contractions: **j'er**, **le'r / la'r / lo'r**, **no'r / no'rn**. 2SG with *tu*: tu'r? / tu er?

- In the corpus *er* is the auxiliary of the past progressive (*ern … pegntant*), the past passive (*er adornað*) and the pluperfect of *star* verbs (*j'er … sortið*). *sta* is the past of *star* with a predicate noun or adjective (*Y grant atelier sta un tapeçment…*, C39).
- **Q3.** Is *er* defective, i.e. only an imperfect, with no present, subjunctive or other forms?
A: Yes. In any other TAM one uses forms of star instead.
- **Q4.** The negative *no'r* also appears with predicate adjectives: *Marc no'r certan de noc faïr…* (C212). Is *no'r* the negative of *sta* as well, or should that be *sta noc* / *no sta*?
A: That should be "no sta", yeah.
