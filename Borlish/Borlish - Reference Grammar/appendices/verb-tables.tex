\chapter{Verb Tables}\label{app:verb-tables}

This appendix gives the full paradigms of the four model verbs of the regular conjugations (Chapter~\ref{ch:verb-morphology}). Except in the future, the singular has a single form for all three persons, which is shown spanning both singular columns.
