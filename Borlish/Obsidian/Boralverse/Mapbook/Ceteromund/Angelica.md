Angelica is a continent of the [[Ceteromund]].

## Name
The name Angelica is first attested in Latin in the eighteenth century, as an alteration of earlier *Angalica* or *Angalaca*, which originally applied only to the continent's northern coast. It came via Arabic and Bāsan from a local language, perhaps Dual Jungle *āngangaraca* "ancestral lands", literally "bone country".

## Polities
- [[New Leudong]], in the south-east, named for the Cathayan peninsula of Leudong. After the Angelican Landfall, emigration from [[Britain]] and state-building from [[Cathay]] formed the modern polity.

## Culture
A variety of Welsh, Angelican Welsh, is spoken on the continent. Pether Hetsong's semi-autobiographical *The Land of Golden Lies and Sugared Fire* (1864) is often considered the first Angelican work of literature to enjoy Vetomundane success.
