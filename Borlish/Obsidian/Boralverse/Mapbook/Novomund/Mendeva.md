Mendeva is the northern of the two continents of the [[Novomund]], between the Atlantic and the Lovac Ocean.

## Name
The name is attested in the adjectival form *mendēvalēnsis* on maps from the 1471 Landfall voyage, and the place name Mendeva was formed backwards from it; the demonym was later rederived as *Mendevānus* "Mendevan". It derives from Vask *mendeval* "west", itself likely a borrowing from Vascon *vent de val* "valley wind".

## Geography
- The [[Bahassassin]], the longest river of the continent
- The [[Sturgovan Mountains]], inland from the eastern coast
- The Five Seas, east of the upper Bahassassin
- The [[Arcabil]], a collection of islands east of Mashick

## Regions
- [[Lower Mendeva]], the southern part of the continent
- [[Gulf Mendeva]], the polities along the Gulf
- [[British Mendeva]], the polities settled from or strongly influenced by the Kingdom of [[Britain]], mostly within Gulf Mendeva

## Polities
- [[Ambrosia]], settled from Vascony and named for King [[Ambrose III of Vascony|Ambrose III]]
- [[New Provence]], a coastal polity grown from trading posts of Provence merchants, with its capital at [[Santrafew]]
- [[Zangathy]], landlocked on the upper Bahassassin
- [[Hasiny]], the largest and most populous polity of British Mendeva, with its capital at Nadacow
- [[Taisha]], a minor polity of Gulf Mendeva with its capital at Yanaquana
- [[Mashick]], by land area the largest polity in Mendeva, descended from the Mashick Empire
- [[Mivock]], a minor coastal polity on the Lovac Ocean
- [[Ax]], a peninsular polity with an island chain running west into the northern Lovac Ocean
- [[Cocain]], the largest island of the Arcabil, together with part of the mainland
- Staddomains, small polities along the eastern coast grown from individual trading posts

## Peoples
Mendevan peoples include the Jaughta, Carovacian, Mastoisèt, Nappe, Guittoc, Esquanna, Daccotan, Miwoc, Oranger, Thebevan, Nawat and Tainow.

## History
- 1471: the [[Novomundane Landfall]] on [[Brasil]] begins prolonged contact with the [[Vetomund]].
- Turn of the nineteenth century: the [[Mendevan Unmooring]] weakens or severs the ties of Mendevan polities to the Vetomund.
- 1920s–30s: the Lovac Wars bring the first major bombing campaigns on civilian targets; Mashick loses northern territory.
