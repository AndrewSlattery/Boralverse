Cappatia is the southern of the two continents of the [[Novomund]], joined to [[Mendeva]] by the Isthmus.

## Name
The name is seen only from the 1570s. European languages take it from Latin *Cappātia*, which comes via Arabic from Run'allo *cappacha* "this world", through contact between Morrack merchants and the Tavancine Empire in the sixteenth century.

## Geography
- The Anticone Mountains
- The Awasú river, inland from the eastern coast across the Camboshung Ridge

## Polities
- [[Tavancy]], a major polity on the western coast between the Isthmus and the Maboutian lands, descended from the Tavancine Empire. The reign of its Emperor Cariwasari (1599–1660) is the longest of any monarch in Cappatian history.
- Maboutia, south of Tavancy
- Dovachine, a minor polity at the southern tip of the continent, named from the Arabic word for "smoke" after the smoke-towers sighted there by Captain al-Casmi in 1513

## Staddomains
Export-based settlements line the spine of eastern Cappatia, grown from trading posts established from the sixteenth century onwards.

- Neuwillem, the first to be established and the most notable of those founded by the Bróderrat, the trading operations of Willemy and its Stadbund cities. Its *Bróderratcronike* records its activities from 1561 to 1597.
- Parnaven, an older seaport
- Varonøw, home of the Nÿstad Atellier
- Awasúconda, upriver on the Awasú across the Camboshung Ridge from Parnaven, a relatively late member of the band
- Monçating, of Borlish influence

Morrack traders of the [[New World Company]] established wayports along the western coast through the sixteenth century, some of which grew into staddomains.

## Peoples
Cappatian peoples and languages include the Run'allo, Warnine, Yengatow and Veonic.
