The Novomund, also called the New World, refers to the lands to the west of the Atlantic Ocean. Inhabited for over a myriad years, the history of these lands dovetails with that of the [[Vetomund]] following the [[Novomundane Landfall]] of 1471.

The Novomund comprises the two great landmasses of [[Mendeva]] and [[Cappatia]], along with several dozen major islands (such as [[Brasil]] and [[Cocain]]) and innumerable lesser ones.

Many crops now cultivated worldwide were first farmed in the Novomund, including shadome fruit, tartover, shockle and mikecorn. Products such as coshow and kistmaw also derive from plants native to the Novomund.

## Name
The name is a univerbation of Latin *novus mundus* "new world"; the phrase *dē novō mundō* "from the new world" is attested from 1509, and Scholastic Latin *novomundus* later supplied the vernacular forms.

## Mendeva
The name Mendeva is attested in the adjectival form *mendēvalēnsis* on maps from the 1471 Landfall voyage. It derives from Vask *mendeval* "west", itself likely a borrowing from Vascon *vent de val* "valley wind".

- Regions: [[Lower Mendeva]], [[Gulf Mendeva]], [[British Mendeva]]
- [[Ambrosia]], settled from Vascony and named for King [[Ambrose III of Vascony|Ambrose III]]
- [[New Provence]], a coastal polity extending westward to the [[Sturgovan Mountains]], grown from trading posts of Provence merchants
- [[Zangathy]], a landlocked polity on the upper [[Bahassassin]], west of the Five Seas
- [[Hasiny]], the largest polity of British Mendeva
- [[Taisha]], a minor polity of Gulf Mendeva at the crossroads of British and Morrack influences
- [[Mashick]], the largest polity of Lower Mendeva, descended from the Mashick Empire
- [[Mivock]], a minor coastal polity on the Lovac Ocean
- [[Ax]], a peninsular polity with an island chain running west into the northern Lovac Ocean
- Staddomains, small polities along the eastern coast grown from individual trading posts

## The Arcabil
*Main article: [[Arcabil]]*

The Arcabil are a collection of islands in the Atlantic, east of Mashick, with a complicated history of influences from various Vetomundane powers. [[Cocain]] is the largest.

## Cappatia
The name Cappatia is seen only from the 1570s. It comes via Latin *Cappātia* and Arabic from Run'allo *cappacha* "this world", through contact between Morrack merchants and the Tavancine Empire.

- [[Tavancy]], a major polity on the western coast, descended from the Tavancine Empire
- Maboutia, south of Tavancy
- Dovachine, a minor polity at the southern tip of the continent
- Staddomains along the eastern coast, among them Neuwillem, Parnaven and Waronøu of Dutch influence and Monçating of Borlish influence
