The Kingdom of the Franks was the realm of the Franks in early medieval [[Gaul]] and beyond. At its greatest it was an expansive empire, the Francian Empire (Borlish **Empir Françan**). After it split in two in the ninth century, the name refers to its western fragment, the West Francian Kingdom, while the eastern half became the [[First German Empire|German Empire]].

## History
### The Francian Empire
The Francian Empire expanded in the sixth century, incorporating [[Provence]] among other lands. Around 720 the Catumagin Accords ended hostilities between the Kingdom of the Franks and the Kernish kingdoms to its west and established an official border between them, part of which ran along the river Vire. This was shortly before [[Vigo the Magnificent|Vigo]] became king of [[Kernow]].

### The division
In the ninth century the empire divided in two. Its eastern half, the Eastern Franks, combined with [[Willemy]], created at the same time, to form the German Empire. The County of [[Avosche]] emerged shortly after the split. The western fragment continued as the Kingdom of the Franks, also called the West Francian Kingdom or West Francia; Provence remained part of it.

### West Francia
In the ninth and tenth centuries the Franks encroached on Kernow from the east, and King Alan III of Greater Devon (crowned 883) lost territory to them. A map of Borland in 900 shows the Western Franks and the Frisians across the sea to the south. Viking raids along the Seine and the Orne began in earnest in the ninth century, and Danes settled across the north of the kingdom. In the tenth century the Franks formally ceded this territory, which became [[Normandy]], with the Vire remaining its western border.

A mapbook of mid-tenth-century Europe shows the West Francian kingdom ruled by Robert I (crowned 923), who held court in [[Paris]]; its other major towns included Reims, Strasbourg, Loyons, Genevre, [[Roun]] and Amiens. The same mapbook shows the neighbouring Kingdom of Aquitain, ruled from Bordew.

By the eleventh century the Duchy of Normandy was a semi-autonomous vassal of the kingdom, ruled from Roun by the Drengot family. After the [[Kernovan Conjuring]] of 1029 the Drengots built the [[First Drengot Empire]], which for most of its existence comprised the kingdoms of West Francia, Wessex, Markland and Dumnonia. Its emperors held court in Paris.

## Language
The Franks spoke Frankish, a German language. Many Frankish words passed into Old French, and through French into Borlish and the English languages. For example, Old French *targe* "small shield" comes from Frankish *targa*.
