Rulers of [[Borland]], from the first Prase of the Diocese of Boral to the dissolution of the monarchy in 1894.

- [[Victor Rossetus]] (fl. 500), first Prase of the Diocese of Boral and nominal ancestor of the royal dynasties of the various Kingdoms of Borland
- [[Stillian of Borland|Stiglan of Borland]] (fl. 810), who unified the First Kingdom of Borland with the Petty Kingdom of Angland
- Marc of Borland (fl. 810), first king of a unified Roman Borland and Angland
- [[Jothey of Borland]] (fl. 920), who fled into exile following the Dane invasion of Borland
- Joseph I (d. 1411), who ruled jointly with his wife
- Natalia I (d. 1416), last of her dynasty, whose death sparked a protracted succession crisis
- [[Joseph III of Borland]] (d. 1534), who ruled through the ravages of the Second Great Dying
- [[Brandon II of Borland]] (fl. 1534), who instituted the land's first terrene tax
- [[Natalia II of Borland|Natalia II]] (abd. 1894), who abdicated the throne as part of Borland accession into the Drengot Collusion
