China (Borlish **Scin**), also called the Southern Empire, is an empire in the east of [[Asia]], south of [[Cathay]]. Its ruler is the Southern Emperor. The two polities have at times been united, but Cathay was permanently sundered from China in the late fourteenth century.

## History
### The age of exploration
News of western exploration reached the Southern Empire around 1470. In 1468 Chinese merchants met the crew of the [[Morrack]] ship *Orion* at Mombasa, and their reports to the Southern Emperor are said to have changed the course of history. Nearly fifty years later Chinese traders guided the British ship *[[Voyage of the Phœbos|Phœbos]]* on to [[Gurjat]], [[Madray]] and [[Borune]], whose port city of Sinquan held an annual fair with a Chinese quarter older than the sultan's conversion to Islam.

In 1520, hearing of the planned circumnavigation of the globe by ships from [[Morrack]] and Britain, the Southern Emperor issued the Hangthen Edict. It declared that if the world could be girdled, China should tie the ropes herself. Chinese ships went on to reach the [[Novomund]], and the sixteenth-century epic of exploration *The Sea Peoples* is considered one of the greatest Chinese works ever written.

### Later history
Several outbreaks of disease in China in the century before 1735 are suspected to have been pennackit, which began its global spread in 1753. In the [[Democratic Wars]] of the mid-eighteenth century, China was embroiled in the contest for control of India. It launched attacks on the eastern Indian polities through one of its protectorates. By the 1820s the European voidtale craze had spread as far as China.

## Language
China has several Chinese languages; etymologies cite a southern, imperial Chinese, and a local language of the coast. Borlish *cocheyau* "stilt" comes, via seventeenth-century travel writing about an acrobatic act in coastal China, from a local word for "stilts" meaning literally "high-tiptoe".
