Honorine Maignard (fl. 1890–1940) was an author in [[France]] active for over fifty years in the early twentieth century. She was the daughter of the [[Lombardy]] diplomat [[Audace Saviano]], who during the [[Good Game]] period was instrumental in the formation of the [[Italian Concert]].

During the [[Millstone War]] she served in the Second Medic Force of Valois for more than six years.
