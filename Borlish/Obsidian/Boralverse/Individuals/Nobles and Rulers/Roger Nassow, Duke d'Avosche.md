Roger Nassow (fl. 1524) was duke of [[Avosche]] in the early sixteenth century.

He married someone called Caroline and with her had eight children (five of which survived infancy), including the future [[Adaillé Nassow, Duchess d'Avosche|Duchess Adaillé]].
