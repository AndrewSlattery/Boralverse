The New Navarre Enterprise (Borlish **Novel Enterpris Navarran**) was a guild and expanded merchant fleet of [[Vascony]], established by royal decree of King [[Ambrose III of Vascony|Ambrose III]] in 1458. Its ships made the [[Novomundane Landfall]] of 1471, bringing news of the [[Novomund]] to Europe.

## Founding
Ambrose III, "the Ascendant", succeeded his grandfather [[Ambrose II of Vascony|Ambrose II]] in 1456 and founded the Enterprise two years later. It had two aims: to secure Vascon supremacy in the trade of North Atlantic fish through the [[Middlesea]], and to create trade ports down the western coast of [[Africa]]. Ambrose wanted Vascony to be not just a hub of trade meshes but a producer of resources in its own right.

British dominion over the Atlantic had long frustrated the Vask fishing families. Their members still predominated in the aristocracy and held significant political power at Ambrose's court. One history therefore calls the Enterprise's founding "a three-fold manœuvre":
- it reinforced Vascony's position as the supreme supplier and distributor of trade goods throughout Europe;
- it gave Ambrose's most powerful vassals an avenue to power and riches that did not threaten his own position;
- though he could not have known it, it set Vascony up to make the most important discovery of the fifteenth century.

## The Landfall
The Enterprise greatly increased Vascon fishing activity in the North Atlantic, and its ships explored the northern Atlantic further than anyone since the Danes. In 1471 Vask ships travelling north past Valland sighted and landed on the island of [[Brasil]], which would go on to host the first European settlements in [[Mendeva]]. A guidebook to Britain accordingly credits, or blames, British fishing for the Landfall, since it was Vascony's wish to displace the British in that trade that led to the Enterprise.

Trade with the Novomund, especially once a far more favourable southern route was found, enriched Vascony, above all the port towns of Portingale and Leon. New foodstuffs reached Europe and North Africa by this route.

## Rivals
In 1463 King [[Munir al-Hamdawi of Morrack|Munir al-Hamdawi]] of [[Morrack]] founded the [[New World Company]] as a rival marine enterprise. Five years later its ship *Orion* circumnavigated Africa.

## In culture
The British film *Hwn a Kyvodoð* (*The Ascendant*), a historical courtly drama directed by Dormaget, tells the story of the years after the Landfall partly through Jata (played by Lyneth Madox), the seamstress wife of an Enterprise sailor in Bilvaw.
